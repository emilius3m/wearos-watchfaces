1. No External Data Collection, Analytics, or Sharing

We do not collect, store, process, transmit, or share any personal data, audio recordings, usage telemetry, location data, or health data on external servers or with third parties. None of our apps include analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or cloud accounts.

2. REC Voice Recorder (it.e3mcorp.rec)

Microphone & Local Storage: REC requests the microphone permission (RECORD_AUDIO) and microphone foreground service permission solely to record audio when initiated by the user, and notification permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) solely to display the ongoing recording status notification. Audio is recorded into local .m4a files stored exclusively inside the app's private storage on your smartwatch and is never uploaded or transmitted off your device automatically. Deleting a recording in the app or uninstalling REC removes those files.

Optional Local Wi-Fi Transfer: Only when you explicitly tap Download to PC, REC starts a temporary, read-only HTTP web page on your local Wi-Fi network so you can copy recordings to your own computer on the same network. Each session uses a randomly generated 4-digit code in the URL shown on your watch and stops automatically when you close the list screen or turn off the toggle. No traffic is ever sent to the internet or external servers.

3. Watch Faces (it.e3mcorp.watchface.*)

All E3M Corp watch faces listed above are built purely with Google's declarative Watch Face Format (WFF 2) (android:hasCode="false") and contain no executable code or network transmission capability. Any watch-face metrics displayed on your watch (time, date, battery level, step count, weather forecasts, or user-configured Wear OS complications) are provided directly on-device by the Wear OS operating system and remain solely on your smartwatch.

4. Children's Privacy

Because these applications do not collect any personal information from anyone, they do not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes to This Policy

If any future update introduces functionality that changes how data is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated before the release is published.
