Privacy Policy — E3M Corp Watch Faces

Effective date: November 13, 2026 · Developer: Emilio Morrone (E3M Corp)

This Privacy Policy applies to the following Wear OS watch face applications distributed on Google Play:

  • NIXIE Watch Face (it.e3mcorp.watchface.nixie)
  • SOLARI Watch Face (it.e3mcorp.watchface.solari)
  • PALETTE Watch Face (it.e3mcorp.watchface.palette)
  • APEX METEO Watch Face (it.e3mcorp.watchface.syshud)

1. No Data Collection or Sharing

All E3M Corp watch faces listed above are built purely with Google's declarative Watch Face Format (WFF 2) (android:hasCode="false"). They contain no executable code, no analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, and no internet or network transmission capability.

We do not collect, store, process, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, location data, or health data on external servers or with third parties.

2. On-Device System Data & Complications

Any watch-face metrics displayed on your watch (such as time, date, battery level, step count, heart rate shortcuts, weather forecasts, or user-configured Wear OS complications) are provided directly on-device by the Wear OS operating system and your chosen system providers. This information remains solely on your smartwatch and is never accessible to or collected by the developer.

3. Children's Privacy

Because these applications do not collect any personal information from anyone, they do not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from children.

4. Changes to This Policy

If any future update introduces functionality that changes how data is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated before the release is published.
